1. Nasdaq, Yahoo and Finviz review: all 30 endpoints, provenance distinctions, company-coverage qualifications, source citations and workbook default costs. Confirmed complete inventory and matching cached scenario values. 2. MarketBeat, StockAnalysis and DefiLlama review: all 29 endpoints, source terms, schema descriptions and product fit. Corrected on-chain data to tokenized-equity/perpetual venue mappings; limited dimensions to documented financial metrics; qualified analyst-rating aggregation and curated stock lists. Premarket polling now has its own window, the StockAnalysis quote panel uses the comparable market polling profile, and MarketBeat's daily overview is explicitly identified as a daily snapshot.
3. Insider/cost review: all 15 insider and ownership endpoints. Confirmed separation into eight shared lists, two per-company insider tools, three per-company institutional tools, one fund-manager query and one on-demand search. Checked cost arithmetic, distinguished fund counts from issuer counts, and required pagination/completeness and conditional caching qualifications. Mechanical all-tool research totals explicitly multiply the number of endpoints used per action.

The parent review incorporated these corrections, reconciled the 70 prices with the saved public catalogue, checked the 55/15 split and every tool card, and checked 560 workbook cost/call cells against default scenario calculations. Document validation also checks native Word footnotes, PDF source numbering, local-path leaks and rendered readability. This is an artifact and evidence review, not a paid endpoint benchmark or security audit.
